
# Zomato-Restaurant-Expansion-Analysis-Project

**Project Overview**
This project focuses on analyzing Zomato's global restaurant dataset using Microsoft Excel to derive insights that support strategic market expansion. The dataset includes over 9,551 restaurants across 15 countries.

**Problem Statement**
1)How can a food delivery platform expand into new markets while ensuring strong brand presence and user adoption?
2)What strategies can improve customer satisfaction in online food delivery compared to dine-in services?
3)What measures can increase customer engagement and repeat usage in low-interaction regions?



**Tools & Techniques**
1)Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, transformation, analysis, and visualization.
2)Functions Used: VLOOKUP, IF, COUNTIFS, AVERAGE, CONCATENATE, CORREL, Date, AVERAGEIF
3)Pivot Tables: For summarizing metrics such as restaurant count by country/year, ratings, and pricing.
4)Charts: Line charts, bar charts, and 3D clustered columns for visualization.

 **Key Insights**
1)Top Expansion Markets: Indonesia, Qatar, Sri Lanka – low competition, high ratings.
2)Service Impact: Online delivery and table booking services significantly boost customer ratings.
3)Cuisine Strategy: International and Indian cuisines receive the highest feedback globally.
4)Engagement: Countries like Canada and Singapore show low interaction, suggesting marketing focus.

**Deliverables**
1)Cleaned and enriched Excel dataset
2)Pivot tables and dynamic dashboard
3)Country-wise and city-wise expansion recommendations
4)Visual charts for trends, customer behavior, and pricing analysis
